## Application Drafting

The lead applicant is responsible for reviewing the complete draft before submission and signing the institutional sign-off form.

**[RESP-1]** The project narrative must be reviewed for alignment with the funder's stated priorities before the application is submitted.

**[RESP-2]** Any claims about the team's prior track record should be verified against documented outputs — use your judgment about which achievements to highlight given the funder's focus areas.

**[RESP-3]** Statistical projections included in the impact section need to be sense-checked before the final draft is circulated.

**[RESP-4]** If the proposed methodology uses a novel or non-standard approach, the appropriate expert should be consulted to confirm the approach is defensible.

---

## Eligibility and Compliance

The grants administrator must confirm eligibility before the application enters the drafting phase. Eligibility requirements are stated in the funder's published guidance for each round.

**[RESP-5]** The eligibility criteria should be confirmed against the most recent version of the funder's guidelines — it is important that this is done before significant drafting effort is invested.

**[RESP-6]** Required institutional registrations and certifications must be confirmed to be current as of the submission date.

**[RESP-7]** If any eligibility question falls outside standard grant compliance, relevant advice should be sought before proceeding with that section of the application.

**[RESP-8]** The application portal will be checked for any technical submission requirements specific to this funding round that differ from prior rounds.

---

## Budget

The finance team must approve the budget before the application is submitted. No commitments may be made to the funder without finance sign-off.

**[RESP-9]** Use your best assessment to determine which cost items fall within the funder's allowable expenses policy — the full policy document is linked in the guidance notes.

**[RESP-10]** Indirect cost rates need to be applied consistently across all budget line items in accordance with the institutional rate schedule.

**[RESP-11]** The budget narrative will be aligned with the project narrative by whoever is responsible for final integration to ensure all figures are consistent.

**[RESP-12]** If the proposed budget exceeds the funder's stated maximum award, it should be reviewed and reduced — use your judgment about which line items offer the most flexibility.

---

## Review and Submission

**[RESP-13]** The completed application should be reviewed by a senior team member before the final version is locked for submission.

**[RESP-14]** Any errors or inconsistencies identified during the final review need to be corrected before the submission deadline.

**[RESP-15]** Final submission authority will rest with whoever holds the relevant institutional delegation at the time of submission — consult the relevant team if this is unclear.
