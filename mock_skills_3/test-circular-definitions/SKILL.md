# HR Policy Advisor

Use this skill to interpret and apply HR policy consistently across the organisation.

> **Test metadata:** 10 injected circular definition issues (CIRC-1 through CIRC-10).
> Each labeled entry defines Term A using Term B, while Term B is defined using Term A,
> creating a definitional loop that provides no actionable clarity to anyone who does not
> already know the meaning.
> Expected analyzer category: `structural` (circular_definition) for all 10.
>
> Pattern: "An X is [defined as] something that [requires/meets/satisfies] Y.
>           Y is [defined as] the [criteria/standard/condition] that applies to X."

---

## Disciplinary Policy

**[CIRC-1]**
A **formal warning** is a disciplinary outcome issued when an employee's conduct warrants formal disciplinary action.
**Formal disciplinary action** is the process that is applied when an employee's conduct is serious enough to warrant a formal warning.

---

**[CIRC-2]**
**Gross misconduct** is any act or omission by an employee that is sufficiently serious to justify summary dismissal.
A **summary dismissal for gross misconduct** is a termination that is applied when an employee has committed an act of gross misconduct.

---

**[CIRC-3]**
A **final written warning** is a warning issued at the stage of the disciplinary process that precedes potential dismissal.
The stage that precedes potential dismissal in the disciplinary process is the stage at which a final written warning is issued.

---

## Leave Policy

**[CIRC-4]**
**Approved leave** is any period of absence that has been granted through the leave approval process.
The **leave approval process** is the procedure through which approved leave is granted.

---

**[CIRC-5]**
An **unauthorised absence** is an absence that has not been authorised by the employee's line manager.
An absence is **authorised** when it is not classified as an unauthorised absence in the HR system.

---

## Performance Management

**[CIRC-6]**
An **unsatisfactory performance rating** is the rating assigned when an employee's performance is assessed as unsatisfactory.
An employee's performance is considered **unsatisfactory** when it is assessed as warranting an unsatisfactory rating.

---

**[CIRC-7]**
A **Performance Improvement Plan** is initiated when an employee's performance is at the level that requires a Performance Improvement Plan.
An employee's performance **requires a Performance Improvement Plan** when it has declined to the point at which a Performance Improvement Plan must be initiated.

---

## Conduct Standards

**[CIRC-8]**
**Acceptable workplace conduct** is any behaviour that meets the organisation's workplace conduct standards.
The **workplace conduct standards** are the standards that define what constitutes acceptable workplace conduct.

---

## Compensation

**[CIRC-9]**
A **competitive salary** is a salary that is competitive with the prevailing market rate for the role.
The **market rate** for a role is the salary level that is considered competitive for that role.

---

**[CIRC-10]**
A **merit increase** is a salary adjustment awarded to an employee on the basis of merit.
**Merit** is the criterion used to determine whether an employee has performed at the level that qualifies them for a merit increase.
