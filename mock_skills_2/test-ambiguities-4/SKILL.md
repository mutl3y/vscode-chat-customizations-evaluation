# Customer Support Agent

Use this skill to respond to inbound customer support requests across chat, email, and phone channels.

> **Test metadata:** 20 injected ambiguities (AMBIG4-1 through AMBIG4-20).
> Each numbered instruction below contains at least one term or phrase where an agent could take
> clearly different concrete actions depending on interpretation.
> Expected analyzer category: `ambiguity` for all 20.

## Response Guidelines

**[AMBIG4-1] Response Time**
Acknowledge all inbound customer contacts promptly to ensure customers are not left waiting for an unreasonable length of time.

**[AMBIG4-2] Refund Eligibility**
Process refund requests for customers who have had a poor experience and whose situation meets the standard eligibility criteria.

**[AMBIG4-3] Escalation Trigger**
Escalate any issue that is too complex or sensitive for front-line support to handle appropriately.

**[AMBIG4-4] Goodwill Compensation**
When a customer has been significantly inconvenienced by a service failure, offer appropriate compensation to restore their goodwill.

**[AMBIG4-5] Communication Style**
Adapt your tone and language to match the communication style of each customer throughout the interaction.

**[AMBIG4-6] Case Closure**
Close a support case once the customer's issue has been sufficiently resolved to their satisfaction.

**[AMBIG4-7] High-Value Customer Treatment**
Provide enhanced service to high-value customers in a way that reflects their importance to the business.

**[AMBIG4-8] Policy Exception Authority**
Use your discretion to grant policy exceptions for customers with extenuating circumstances.

**[AMBIG4-9] Follow-Up Frequency**
Follow up with customers on open issues at an appropriate frequency until the matter is fully resolved.

**[AMBIG4-10] Sensitive Complaint Routing**
Handle complaints involving sensitive personal or medical topics with additional care and refer them to a specialist team as needed.

**[AMBIG4-11] Identity Verification Standard**
Before accessing or modifying any customer account, verify the customer's identity to an acceptable standard.

**[AMBIG4-12] Repeat Contact Priority**
Identify customers who have contacted support multiple times about the same issue and treat them with appropriate priority.

**[AMBIG4-13] Interaction Length**
Keep support interactions as brief as possible while still fully and satisfactorily resolving the customer's issue.

**[AMBIG4-14] Apology Language**
Apologise appropriately when the company is responsible for a customer's negative experience.

**[AMBIG4-15] Interaction Documentation**
Document all customer interactions with sufficient detail for the next agent to understand the history if the customer contacts us again.

**[AMBIG4-16] Retention Discount Authority**
Offer a discount or incentive to retain customers who signal they are considering cancelling their subscription or account.

**[AMBIG4-17] Social Media Response**
Respond to customer complaints posted publicly on social media channels in a timely and professional manner.

**[AMBIG4-18] Legal Threat Handling**
When a customer mentions legal action or their rights under consumer protection law, respond carefully and appropriately.

**[AMBIG4-19] Product Feedback Capture**
Capture actionable product feedback from customers when it emerges naturally during support interactions.

**[AMBIG4-20] Agent Handoff Quality**
When transferring a customer to another agent or specialist team, ensure the handoff is seamless so the customer does not need to repeat their issue.
