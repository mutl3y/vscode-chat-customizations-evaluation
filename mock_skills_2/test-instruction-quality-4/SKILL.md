## Writing Instructions

### [QUALITY4-1] Engagement Quality
When writing body copy for any marketing channel, try to make it engaging and relevant to the intended audience.

---

### [QUALITY4-2] Brand Voice
All copy generated by this skill should align with the brand's established voice and tone guidelines.

---

### [QUALITY4-3] Call to Action
For campaign emails and landing pages, you might want to include a clear call-to-action if it seems appropriate for the piece.

---

### [QUALITY4-4] Pre-Approval Requirement
Do not generate copy that is not unsuitable for publication unless it has not been excluded from pre-approval requirements by the marketing director.

---

### [QUALITY4-5] Copy Review Gate
All generated copy will be reviewed by the brand team for accuracy and regulatory compliance before it is published to any channel.

---

### [QUALITY4-6] Headline Style
Write headlines in a style something like this: "Discover Why Thousands Choose Us" — or whatever phrasing feels right for the product and moment.

---

### [QUALITY4-7] Channel Tone
Use your judgment to determine the appropriate tone for each channel — email, social, paid search, and in-product copy may each need a different register and level of formality.

---

### [QUALITY4-8] Regulated Claims
If a claim requires legal or regulatory review before it can be published, consult the brand team as appropriate before including it in the final copy.

---

### [QUALITY4-9] Social Proof Elements
Consider whether including a customer testimonial, review quote, or social proof statistic would strengthen this piece of copy.

---

### [QUALITY4-10] Email Subject Line Length
All email subject lines must be exactly 47 characters long, measured after all personalisation tokens have been resolved to their final values.

---

### [QUALITY4-11] Social Media Formatting
For all social media copy, include a version formatted for the Twitter 280-character limit with hashtag placement annotated.

---

### [QUALITY4-12] Urgency Language
In some cases it may sometimes be worth considering whether adding urgency language such as "Limited time offer" or "Offer ends Friday" would improve conversion for this campaign.

---

### [QUALITY4-13] Copy Length
Keep all copy concise and to the point — every additional word reduces engagement and dilutes the core message.

Every piece of campaign copy must fully articulate all relevant value propositions, differentiators, and supporting proof points so that a reader encountering the brand for the first time can make a fully informed decision.

---

### [QUALITY4-14] Campaign Copy Generation
Generate the full campaign copy suite for this launch: email sequence, social media posts, landing page headline and subheadline, and paid search ad copy.

*Note: Before writing any copy, first conduct audience research to understand the target segment's language, pain points, and motivations — this research is the foundation for all effective marketing copy.*

---

### [QUALITY4-15] Copy Length by Format
Adjust the length of each piece of copy as needed based on the format and context.
